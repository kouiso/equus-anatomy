EQUUS Horse Anatomy helps you learn how a horse's body is built by exploring illustrated anatomy charts.

FOUR VIEWS, FOUR LAYERS
Look at the horse from the left, right, front and rear, and switch between the skin, muscle, skeleton and organ layers. Pinch or use the buttons to zoom in on the details.

TAP A PART TO LEARN IT
Tap any part on the illustration to see its name and a short explanation, so you can connect what you see with what it is called.

CATALOG AND SEARCH
Browse every part in the catalog, or search for the one you are looking for.

MARK AND SAVE
Mark parts you have learned and save the ones you want to review later. Your progress stays on your device.

NO NETWORK, NO ACCOUNT
No sign-up is needed. The app sends no data anywhere and contains no ads or analytics. It works offline.

PLEASE NOTE
The app is currently available in Japanese only. It is a study aid: the anatomical accuracy of the illustrations and notes has not yet been reviewed by a veterinarian. Do not use it for diagnosis or treatment decisions.
